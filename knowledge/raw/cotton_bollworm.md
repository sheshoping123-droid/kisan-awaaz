# Cotton Bollworm Management

## Overview
The cotton bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera) is a major pest of cotton in Pakistan.
Larvae feed on buds, flowers, and bolls, causing significant yield loss.

## Identification
- Eggs: small, white, laid singly on leaves and buds
- Larvae: green to brown with dark stripes, 3-4 cm when mature
- Damage: holes in bolls, premature boll opening, stained lint

## Economic Threshold
Spray when larvae count reaches 5-8 per 25 plants in early season,
or 10-15 per 25 plants during peak flowering.

## Management Strategies

### Cultural Control
- Early sowing (May) to avoid peak bollworm population
- Use trap crops (maize, sorghum) around cotton fields
- Destroy crop residues after final picking

### Biological Control
- Conserve natural enemies: Trichogramma wasps, ladybird beetles
- Apply HaNPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) at 250 LE/ha

### Chemical Control
- First spray: chlorantraniliprole or spinosad
- Alternate chemistry to prevent resistance
- Avoid spraying during peak flowering to protect pollinators
