Wheat Rust Disease Management

Wheat rust is one of the most destructive diseases affecting wheat crops in Pakistan.
There are three main types: yellow rust (stripe rust), brown rust (leaf rust), and black rust (stem rust).

Symptoms:
Yellow rust appears as yellow-orange pustules in stripes along the leaf.
Brown rust shows as small, circular, orange-brown pustules scattered on leaves.
Black rust produces dark reddish-brown pustules on stems and leaf sheaths.

Management:
Plant resistant varieties such as Faisalabad-2008, Galaxy-2013, or Pakistan-2013.
Apply fungicide sprays (propiconazole or tebuconazole) at the first sign of disease.
Remove crop residue after harvest to reduce inoculum carryover.
Monitor fields regularly during cool, humid weather when rust spreads fastest.

Prevention:
Use certified seed from approved sources.
Avoid late sowing, as late-planted wheat is more vulnerable.
Maintain balanced fertilizer application — excess nitrogen increases susceptibility.
